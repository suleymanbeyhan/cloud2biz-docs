## Opening a Support Ticket

For technical issues, quota increases, new flavor requests or API access, open a support ticket in your client area. To get a quick resolution:

- Specify the related **service** (e.g. *Flexostack - Sabit Kaynak - İstanbul*).
- Describe when the problem started and what you were doing when it happened.
- Include details such as the virtual machine name, IP address or bucket name.
- Attach screenshots of error messages or the screen.

:::warning For your security
Do not share passwords, the secret part of access keys (secret access key) or card details in support tickets. The Cloud2Biz team will never ask you for them.
:::
