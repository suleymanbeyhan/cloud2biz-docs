## Payment Methods

You can pay in the client area by **credit or debit card**, through a secure virtual POS.

## Invoices

- You can view and pay your invoices in the **Ödeme** (Payment) section of the client area or on the **Fatura** (Invoice) tab of the service page.
- Invoices are issued with the billing details in **Hesap Detayları** (Account details). If your company name, tax details or address change, update them before the invoice is issued.
- Unless stated otherwise, prices are in **USD** and exclude taxes.

## Billing Models

FlexoStack services are billed with two models:

| Model | Description | Details |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Fixed Resources** | Resources are invoiced in advance for monthly, 6-month or 12-month periods; longer periods are discounted. | [Fixed Resources](/en/docs/flexostack-cloud/faturalandirma/sabit-kaynak) |
| **PAYG** | Resources are metered hourly; you pay for what you use. | [PAYG](/en/docs/flexostack-cloud/faturalandirma/payg) |

To estimate costs, use the [Fixed Resources Calculator](/en/docs/flexostack-cloud/faturalandirma/fiyat-hesaplayici) and the [PAYG Calculator](/en/docs/flexostack-cloud/faturalandirma/payg-hesaplayici).

## Package Changes {#package-changes}

- **Increasing resources:** In the client area, go to **Hizmetler** > the service > package upgrade to increase resources instantly by paying the additional fee.
- **Decreasing resources:** You can decrease resources from the same screen. No refund is made for the current period; next month's invoice is reduced accordingly.

FlexoStack details: [Resource Changes](/en/docs/flexostack-cloud/faturalandirma/sabit-kaynak#resource-changes).
