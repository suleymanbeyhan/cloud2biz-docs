You need a **customer account** to buy and manage Cloud2Biz services. All your services, invoices and support tickets are managed under this account.

## Creating a New Account

1. Go to [cloud2biz.com/kayitol](https://cloud2biz.com/kayitol).
2. Fill in your personal or company details and contact information completely in the registration form.
3. Submit the form. Once your account is created, you can log in to the [client area](https://cloud2biz.com/clientarea).

:::tip
Make sure your billing details (company name, tax office and number, address) are correct. Your invoices are issued with these details.
:::

## Logging In to the Client Area

Log in to the client area at [cloud2biz.com/clientarea](https://cloud2biz.com/clientarea) with the email address and password you set during registration.

For how to use the client area, see [Client Area](./musteri-paneli.md).

## Account Security

- Use a strong password that you use only for your Cloud2Biz account.
- Manage your account's security settings in the **Güvenlik** (Security) section of the client area.
- Instead of sharing your password with colleagues who need access, add separate users in the **Yetkililer** (Authorized users) section.
