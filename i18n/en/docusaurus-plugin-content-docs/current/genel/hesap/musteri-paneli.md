The **client area** ([cloud2biz.com/clientarea](https://cloud2biz.com/clientarea)) is where you manage your Cloud2Biz account, services and payments.

## Sections of the Client Area

The left menu has two sections:

| Section | Menu | Contents |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Yönet** (Manage) | **Kontrol Paneli** (Dashboard) | Overview of your account |
| | **Hizmetler** (Services) | All services you have purchased and their details |
| **Hesap** (Account) | **Hesap Detayları** (Account details) | Your personal / company details and billing details |
| | **Yetkililer** (Authorized users) | Additional users who can access your account |
| | **Ödeme** (Payment) | Payments and balance |
| | **Güvenlik** (Security) | Account security settings |

The top menu has **Arama** (Search), the **Satın Al** (Buy) button for new services, the shopping cart, notifications and your account balance.

## Service Details

When you select a service under **Hizmetler**, the service page opens. A FlexoStack service, for example, has these tabs:

| Tab | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| **Giriş Detayları** (Login details) | Service panel URL, username and password |
| **Sunucu Listesi** (Server list) | Servers under the service |
| **Kaynaklar** (Resources) | Resources assigned to the service |
| **Fatura** (Invoice) | Invoices for the service |

For logging in to FlexoStack, see [Logging In to the FlexoStack Panel](/en/docs/flexostack-cloud/ilk-adimlar/panele-giris).

## Common Tasks

| Task | How |
| --- | --- |
| Buying a new service | **Satın Al** in the top menu |
| Increasing / decreasing service resources | **Hizmetler** > the service > package upgrade. See [Payments and Invoices](../odeme/odeme-ve-faturalar.md#package-changes) |
| Viewing and paying invoices | **Ödeme** section or the **Fatura** tab on the service page |
| Updating billing details | **Hesap Detayları** |
| Opening a support ticket | See [Support and Contact](../destek/destek-ve-iletisim.md) |
