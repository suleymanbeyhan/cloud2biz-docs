## How the calculation works

- **Unit price:** The monthly unit price for the selected billing period is used. Unit prices are lower for 6- and 12-month prepayment.
- **Quantity discount:** For vCPU, RAM, disk and backup storage, once the quantity reaches a tier, the discount applies to the whole line:

| Resource | 10% discount | 15% discount |
| --- | --- | --- |
| vCPU | 9–32 | 33 and above |
| RAM | 33–64 GB | 65 GB and above |
| Disk and backup storage | 4–20 TB | 20 TB and above |

- **Period total:** Monthly total × period length (1, 6 or 12 months).
- **Windows Server license:** Enter the number of virtual machines that will run Windows Server; the license is 25 USD per virtual machine per month regardless of vCPU count.

Network traffic, Object Storage, SSL and taxes are not included. For all unit prices and billing rules, see [Fixed Resources Billing](./sabit-kaynak.md).
